# What the product has to know

Sentryfi is sold to people who cannot afford an accountant. That only works if the product knows the things an accountant knows, and says them plainly at the moment they matter. This folder is that knowledge, written down so it can be checked, argued with and corrected — not carried in somebody's head or in a chat history.

Started 22 September 2026, when the owner asked for expert depth across everything the product covers.

## The references

| File | What it settles |
|---|---|
| `accounting-standards.md` | The standards a small company's books must satisfy: inventory, assets, revenue, leases, foreign currency, receivables, and the month-end adjustments that make a period true. |
| `imports-and-trade.md` | Incoterms, customs valuation, how freight is priced and spread (FCL, LCL by CBM, air by chargeable weight), payment methods (TT, LC, D/P, D/A, credit, batches), bank charges and the FX spread, and landed cost in practice. |
| `maldives-tax-and-statutory.md` | GST at 8% and 17%, when it is charged and what can be claimed, customs duty on CIF and the fees that apply even when duty does not, income tax, withholding, green tax, pension, and what records must be kept. |
| `international-tax-and-einvoicing.md` | What a country pack must carry, how import VAT differs from the Maldivian model, and the electronic invoicing regimes a product must satisfy to be sold abroad. |
| `industry-mechanics.md` | How the money works in construction, real estate, engineering, travel, freelance, retail, wholesale and plant hire — and which parts are genuinely different rather than differently named. |
| `money-borrowed-and-paid.md` | Every kind of borrowing a small business meets, how a repayment splits, what banks charge, payroll's shape, and expense management as a discipline. |

## How these are used

1. **They decide what the product asks.** A question the app puts to a person exists because one of these says the answer changes the books.
2. **They are the source of the sentence beside the figure.** The product teaches as it goes; what it teaches comes from here, not from invention.
3. **They are reviewed, not trusted.** Every claim carries its source. Anything a Maldivian accountant must confirm is listed as such at the end of the file that raises it, and stays listed until somebody qualified signs it off.
4. **They go stale.** Each file carries the date it was last researched. Rates and mandates change; a figure older than a year is a question, not a fact.

## The rule that does not bend

Knowing the rule is not permission to apply it silently. The product may compute, suggest and explain. A person decides, and the trail ends at their name.
