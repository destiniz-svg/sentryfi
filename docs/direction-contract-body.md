# Surface brief: Sentryfi app (phone flow and desktop shell)

Scope: the Sentryfi app shell and its screens, built first as interactive design artboards under `design/project/` (App.dc.html: Home, Snap, Review, Recorded as one stateful flow; Desktop.dc.html: the Accountant / CFO journal shell; Brand.dc.html: the brand sheet). Visitor mode: Operate. Audience, job, proof, and constraints: `docs/shape-app-shell.md`. Not repeated here.

Unresolved: External auditor role confirmation; funding treatment, excavator ownership, and MIRA template wording (accountant); Dhivehi.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The books as a site notice board, readable at arm's length in equatorial sun. It refuses the dark bento dashboard of rounded cards with a glowing accent, and it refuses metric-card grids; every amount hangs on one vertical rule.

OWN-WORLD: Signal yellow #F2C300 fields on a white board with ink #141414; concrete #6B7078 for secondary text; money in #167A41, money out #C62B20. Barlow Condensed 700 for display and labels, Barlow 500 and 600 for body and tabular numerals. Components: the band (a full-width yellow field for the one number or action that matters now), the chevron strip (ink with yellow hazard chevrons, deadlines only), the rule (a 2px ink vertical line amounts right-align to, with hairline graduations per row), the graduated bar (ticks every 10 percent), the black strip (the app's single voice for confirmation, undo, and warnings), the shutter (an ink disc inside a yellow ring), and a tinted ink glass bar for navigation only. Square corners on fields; 8px grid.

STORY: The Owner sees today's position in one glance, the next deadline in the next, and the bill he is holding is recorded in three taps. He believes the books are right because the confirmation shows the balanced journal, the archive filename, and an undo that lasts ten seconds. He returns to Home and watches the total re-count.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone 390 by 844. Top: company name in condensed caps with a switcher chevron, the role tag, the mark. Then the yellow band, 160px tall: "CASH & BANK · TODAY", the total in 64px condensed numerals, and a line for bank, cash, and sync time. Then the 40px chevron strip: "GST DUE IN 9 DAYS · MVR 21,480 · FILE BY 28 SEP". Then the ruled list "LATEST": five rows of date, who and what, amount on the rule. Then the project rule for Hotel · 48 rooms with the graduated bar at 62 percent. Bottom: the floating tinted glass bar with Home, Bills, the shutter raised at centre, Cash, More.

FORM: Site Board, construction signage and survey instruments, candidate 3 of 7 on the ordered grounded list (after the carbon daybook and the atoll chart), assigned by seed key 49afc33d and chosen by the owner over the pick, the current Ink & Gold artboards, the Datamatics challenger, and the canon. Signature interaction: confirm posts the entry, Recorded shows the balanced journal with a ten-second undo, and returning Home slides the new row onto the rule while the band's total counts down to the new figure. Motion grammar: one exponential ease-out moment per screen change, nothing decorative.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
