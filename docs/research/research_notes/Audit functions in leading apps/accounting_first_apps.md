# Audit functions in leading small-business accounting apps (Xero, QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books, Sage, mybooksai)

## How is auditor/accountant access modelled, and can it expire automatically?

### Takeaway
All four major platforms model external accountant access as a distinct, invited role attached to the business's own file (not a separate copy of the data), with Xero and QuickBooks giving accountants a dedicated "read-mostly-but-powerful" role plus firm-level portals (Xero HQ/Practice Manager, QuickBooks Online Accountant). None of the vendor documentation found describes automatic time-boxed/self-expiring auditor access — access persists until an admin manually removes the user. mybooksai's own audit-specific access model could not be confirmed from public sources.

### Cited Findings
- Xero's **Adviser (Advisor)** user role gives an external accountant/bookkeeper broad access to view and work with financial data (including setting lock dates) without letting them manage other users or billing; a separate, more restrictive **Read Only** role excludes inventory, multicurrency, payroll and settings entirely — [Xero Central: User roles and permissions](https://central.xero.com/0/article/User-roles-and-permissions-in-Xero-Business-edition-GL); [Xero Central: The administrator/adviser user role explained](https://central.xero.com/0/article/Adviser-user-role) (fetch of this exact page returned empty content at time of research, so role details above are drawn from the roles-and-permissions article and secondary summaries).
- Xero accountants/bookkeepers manage all their clients from **Xero HQ** and **Xero Practice Manager**, a firm-level practice management layer separate from each client's own Xero organisation — [Xero: Xero HQ](https://xero.com/us/features-and-tools/practice-tools/xero-hq); [Xero Central: About clients and contacts (Xero HQ)](https://central.xero.com/0/article/Clients-in-Xero-HQ).
- QuickBooks Online: a company admin invites an accountant via **Settings → Manage users → Accounting Firms tab → Invite firm**, entering the accountant's email or firm user ID; the accountant's status shows "Invited" until they accept, then "Active." Accountant users do **not** count toward the subscription's user limit — [Intuit: Invite accountant users](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-global/help-article/account-management/managing-accountant-users-quickbooks-online/L2AcdYvHw_ROW_en) (secondary summaries corroborate; the underlying help-article fetch errored during this research).
- QuickBooks accountant users get elevated "Accountant Tools" privileges (batch reclassify, undo bulk reconciliation, write off invoices) once invited into **QuickBooks Online Accountant (QBOA)**, the firm-side hub — [Webgility: How to Reclassify Transactions in QBO](https://www.webgility.com/blog/how-to-reclassify-transactions-in-quickbooks-online); [Intuit: Accountant tools: Reclassify transactions](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/reclassify-transactions/accountant-tools-reclassify-clients-transactions/L5ZJQODLR_US_en_US).
- Zoho Books manages external accountants through an **Accountant** module/role inside the business's own organisation (not a separate portal), used for journal entries, reconciliation, and **Transaction Locking**, accessible from the left sidebar under "Accountant" — [Zoho Books Help: Accountant](https://www.zoho.com/us/books/help/accountant/accountant.html); [Zoho Books Help: Transaction Locking](https://www.zoho.com/us/books/help/accountant/transaction-lock.html).
- Sage 50 (desktop) controls per-user access via **Maintain → Users → Set Up Security** (Premium: per-user rights; Quantum: role-based "Roles" tab), including a specific checkbox to grant/deny a user the right to override the lock date — [Sage KB: Configure users access to Lock Date](https://gb-kb.sage.com/portal/app/portlets/results/viewsolution.jsp?solutionid=250331094252220); [Sage Help: Security Access Rights](https://help-sage50.na.sage.com/en-ca/AcctEd/2024/Content/System_Settings/Security/SecurityAccessRights.htm).
- mybooksai (mybooksai.app, by Zetran Corporation) is marketed as an AI-driven small-business/freelancer accounting and billing app (GST invoicing, inventory, cash flow, an "AI Accounting Agent"); its own marketing claims only that its reports "make it easy to share insights with...auditors and regulatory authorities" — no dedicated auditor/accountant role, invitation flow, or audit-log feature was found in public sources — [Zetran: Introducing myBooks AI](https://zetran.com/introducing-mybooks-ai-the-future-of-accounting-software/); [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mybooksai-billing-accounting/id6748947230). (mybooksai.com being a parked domain, per the assignment brief, was not independently re-verified in this research pass but no live product content was found there either.)

### Inferences
- Xero and QuickBooks both separate "the accountant's own workspace" (Xero HQ/Practice Manager; QuickBooks Online Accountant) from "the client's ledger," letting one accountant login reach many clients — this is the modern pattern for multi-client external audit/bookkeeping firms, whereas Zoho Books keeps the accountant role inside each client company rather than in a firm-wide portal.
- None of the surveyed vendors appear to offer a **self-expiring** or time-boxed external-access grant (e.g., "access valid for 30 days then auto-revokes") — access control is binary (invited/active vs removed), placing the burden of offboarding on the business admin.

### Gaps
- No official documentation found describing automatic expiry of accountant/adviser access in any of the four established apps.
- Could not confirm from primary sources whether Zoho Books' Accountant role can be scoped read-only vs. full-edit at invite time (found only that the module exists); Zoho's own accountant/FAQ pages would need direct fetch to confirm granular permission toggles.
- mybooksai's actual in-product access model (roles, invitations) is not documented publicly beyond app-store marketing copy; this is a genuine information gap, not an omission.

## How are closed periods locked, and how are edits after close shown (audit log detail, before/after values)?

### Takeaway
Xero and Sage both implement true period locking that blocks posting/edits before a cutoff date, with Xero uniquely offering two lock levels (adviser-only "soft" lock vs. everyone "hard" lock); QuickBooks Online instead uses a closing date that is a soft deterrent (optionally password-protected) and specifically logs any post-close edits to an **Exceptions to Closing Date** report; Zoho Books' Transaction Locking is module-level (Sales/Purchases/Banking/Accounts) and logs unlock events by user. All four maintain some form of change log showing who/when, but explicit granular "before value → after value" diff views are most clearly documented for Zoho Books and Xero's History & Notes; QuickBooks' audit log shows original transaction details but format detail beyond that wasn't confirmed in the sources found.

### Cited Findings
- **Xero** has two lock dates functioning as a soft and hard close: the **Adviser lock date** blocks standard users but lets Adviser-role users keep posting/adjusting; the **End-of-period (hard) lock date** blocks every user, advisers included, once the period is final. Only users with the Adviser role can set or change either lock date, and after a lock date is set, users can't add/edit transactions dated on or before it (though drafts can still be created/submitted for approval) — [Xero Central: Set up or remove lock dates](https://central.xero.com/0/article/Set-up-and-work-with-lock-dates); [Bookkeeping Tutor: Lock Dates in Xero](https://www.bookkeepingtutor.com.au/blog/lock-dates-in-xero/); [Chargebee KB: Understanding Lock Dates in Xero](https://www.chargebee.com/docs/billing/2.0/kb/accounting/xero-lock-dates).
- Xero's **History and Notes** report (sometimes called an "audit trail"/"audit log") records changes to invoices, bills, contacts, inventory items, fixed assets, and organisation financial settings, date-stamped with the user who made the change; the History and Notes section on the financial settings page specifically records every lock-date change and who made it, and best practice is to add a note explaining why a period was unlocked — [Xero Central: History and notes report](https://central.xero.com/0/article/View-a-history-and-notes-summary-for-transactions-and-user-activity) (page fetch errored; summary drawn from search-result excerpts of this and related Xero Central pages); [Bookkeeping Tutor: Lock Dates in Xero](https://www.bookkeepingtutor.com.au/blog/lock-dates-in-xero/).
- A Xero customer/product-ideas thread confirms users have explicitly requested a **comprehensive client audit trail in Practice Manager** (the firm-level tool) because one doesn't yet exist there for accountability of changes to client records — a Xero product team reply noted this "wasn't included in initial work but is something the team would like to look at longer term," i.e., an acknowledged current gap — [Xero Product Ideas: Practice Manager - Audit trail for client history](https://productideas.xero.com/forums/940636-for-accountants-bookkeepers/suggestions/47575646-practice-manager-audit-trail-for-client-history).
- **QuickBooks Online**: admins set a closing date via the "Close the books" toggle, optionally requiring a password before changes before/on that date can be made ("Allow changes after viewing a warning and entering password"). Only admins or the QuickBooks Online Accountant user can change the closing date/password — [Intuit: Set/change closing date and password (Desktop context, closely related to QBO's model)](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/close-books/set-change-closing-date-closing-date-password/L2uKHmMhZ_US_en_US); [Intuit: Edit your closed books in QuickBooks Online](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/customer-company-settings/edit-closed-books/L76xHuaZ5_US_en_US).
- Any transaction changed before/through the QBO closing date is captured in the **Exceptions to Closing Date report**, listing all changes made to transactions dated before/including the closing date, whenever those edits happened — [Intuit: Edit your closed books in QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/customer-company-settings/edit-closed-books/L76xHuaZ5_US_en_US).
- QBO's general **Audit Log** displays date of change, the user who made it, and original transaction details, and tracks not just financial transactions but all account activity — sign-ins, settings changes, edits to customers/vendors/employees, payroll submissions — [Intuit: Use the audit log in QuickBooks Online](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/audit-log/use-audit-log-quickbooks-online/L2WoVnW6I_US_en_US) (page fetch errored directly; content drawn from search-result excerpt of this exact official page).
- QBO audit log retention is contested in sources: Intuit's own help content (as excerpted via search) states events are available for **2 years**, while a January 2022 Intuit product update announced retention would extend to **7 years**; secondary guidance recommends exporting the log to CSV/Excel periodically regardless, since the in-product retention window is finite — [Intuit help article via search excerpt](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/audit-log/use-audit-log-quickbooks-online/L2WoVnW6I_US_en_US); [Firm of the Future: QBO new features Jan 2022](https://www.firmofthefuture.com/product-update/quickbooks-online-new-features-and-improvements-january-2022/). This is a direct conflict between sources that could not be resolved by direct fetch in this pass.
- **Zoho Books Transaction Locking** freezes transactions dated on/before a chosen lock date so they can't be added, modified, or deleted; it can be applied per module (Sales, Purchases, Banking, Accounts) or across all at once. If a locked period is later unlocked, that unlock action itself is written into the activity log together with the user's name — [Zoho Books Help: Transaction Locking](https://www.zoho.com/us/books/help/accountant/transaction-lock.html); [Zoho Books FAQ: What is Transaction Locking?](https://www.zoho.com/us/books/kb/accountant/acc-transactionlock.html).
- Zoho Books' broader **Audit Trail** logs every change to any record (contact, sales/purchase transaction, invoice, bill, journal, even settings), stores a timestamp and the acting user, and explicitly **cannot be disabled**, which the vendor ties to regulatory (e.g., Indian MCA) audit-trail mandates. Crucially, it supports version comparison: the system shows the current version of a transaction alongside a list of prior versions so an auditor/user can see **exactly what changed, by whom, and when** — [Zoho Books: Why audit-trail with Zoho Books?](https://zoho.com/in/books/audit-trail/); [Zoho Books FAQ: Does Zoho Books have Audit Trail?](https://www.zoho.com/us/books/kb/reports/audit-trail.html); [Zoho Blog: Audit trail compliance in Zoho Books](https://www.zoho.com/blog/books/audit-trail-compliance-in-zoho-books.html).
- **Sage 50** (Premium Accounting and higher) has a built-in **Audit Trail** report; separately, period locking is configured via **Settings → Access Rights**, where an admin can tick/untick a "Lock date settings" checkbox per user to allow or block that user from overriding the lock date. Once a period is locked, no new transactions can post to it and existing transactions in it cannot be edited or deleted — [Sage Help: Using Sage 50's Audit Trail](https://help-sage50.na.sage.com/en-us/2019/Content/Reports_Forms/FinancialReports/Use_Audit_Trail.htm); [Sage KB: Configure users access to Lock Date](https://gb-kb.sage.com/portal/app/portlets/results/viewsolution.jsp?solutionid=250331094252220); [Sage Community Hub: Sage 50 Accounts Lock Date](https://communityhub.sage.com/gb/sage-50-accounts/f/general-discussion-uk/230086/sage-50-accounts-lock-date).
- Sage additionally runs an automatic integrity check: **the system performs a full audit whenever the default posting period changes or the fiscal year is closed**, logging any accounting issues it finds for review — [G2: Sage Business Cloud Accounting Features](https://www.g2.com/products/sage-business-cloud-accounting/features) (secondary aggregator; not independently verified against a primary Sage help page in this pass).

### Inferences
- Xero's two-tier (soft/hard) lock date is the most granular period-lock mechanism found among these apps, deliberately built to let the accountant keep adjusting after the wider team is frozen out — a workflow shape the other three apps approximate less precisely (QBO's password-gated closing date is softer/bypassable by anyone who has the password; Zoho's locking is module-scoped rather than adviser-vs-everyone scoped).
- QuickBooks Online's model treats the closing date primarily as a deterrent-plus-exception-report rather than a hard block, which is a materially different tamper-evidence philosophy from Xero/Sage/Zoho's true "no edits possible" locks — auditors relying on QBO need the Exceptions to Closing Date report specifically, not just the general audit log.
- Zoho Books appears to have the most explicit "cannot be disabled" audit-trail guarantee among the four, likely driven by it needing to meet statutory audit-trail mandates (e.g., India's MCA rule) that Xero/QBO/Sage are not documented as being bound by in the same way.

### Gaps
- Could not directly confirm QuickBooks Online audit log's exact display format for "before vs after" field-level values (the search excerpt says it shows "original transaction details" but the primary help page itself could not be fetched in this pass to confirm format/granularity).
- Sage Business Cloud Accounting (the newer cloud SaaS product, distinct from Sage 50 desktop) has much thinner public documentation on period locking and accountant-specific access than Sage 50; most sources found were Sage 50-specific. This is a real product-documentation gap, not a research shortfall — Sage Business Cloud Accounting's own audit trail report exists (per G2 and general Sage KB pages) but its period-lock and accountant-role mechanics specifically were not confirmed from primary Sage documentation.
- Exact QBO audit log retention window remains unresolved (2 years vs 7 years, conflicting sources above).

## What "audit pack" style exports exist (trial balance, GL, ageing, fixed assets, bank recs)?

### Takeaway
QuickBooks Online Accountant has the most purpose-built "audit pack" workflow via **Prep for Taxes** (formerly Trial Balance), combining P&L, balance sheet, prior-year comparison, and adjusting entries in one export-ready dashboard, including direct export/mapping to ProConnect Tax Online; Xero and Zoho Books rely more on their standard report set (trial balance, GL, aged receivables/payables, fixed asset reports) plus their History/Notes or Audit Trail reports rather than a single bundled "audit pack" export; Sage's Audit Trail report is transaction-history-focused rather than a full audit-pack bundle. None of the sources found describe a single-click bundled export combining TB + GL + ageing + fixed assets + bank rec in one document for any of the four apps.

### Cited Findings
- QuickBooks Online Accountant's **Prep for Taxes** (successor to the old Trial Balance tool) shows a client's profit & loss and balance sheet side by side, with prior-year totals, account differences, and adjusting journal entries made during review, and lets the accountant make on-screen adjustments to any category without leaving the workflow — [Intuit Tax Pro Center: Trial Balance in QBOA Reimagined to Prep for Taxes](https://accountants.intuit.com/taxprocenter/proconnect/trial-balance-in-qboa-reimagined-to-prep-for-taxes/); [Intuit: Introducing Prep for taxes](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-au/help-article/manage-client/introducing-prep-taxes/L46ItXRic_AU_en_AU) (direct fetch of this exact page errored; summary drawn from search-result excerpt).
- Prep for Taxes also supports **mapping and exporting a client's tax-relevant account data directly to ProConnect Tax Online**, i.e., a structured export pipeline from the accounting file into the tax-prep/audit-support workflow — [Intuit: Use Prep for taxes to map and export your clients' tax info](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/map-forms-accounts/use-prep-taxes-map-export-clients-tax-info/L4EUJdqX3_US_en_US).
- QuickBooks Online Accountant's **Accountant Tools** (briefcase icon) include batch **Reclassify Transactions** (bulk-move transactions between accounts/classes/locations) and tools to undo bulk reconciliations and batch-write off invoices — useful for audit-adjustment workflows even though these aren't literally "export" features — [Webgility: How to Reclassify Transactions in QuickBooks Online](https://www.webgility.com/blog/how-to-reclassify-transactions-in-quickbooks-online); [Intuit: Accountant tools: Reclassify transactions](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/reclassify-transactions/accountant-tools-reclassify-clients-transactions/L5ZJQODLR_US_en_US).
- Xero's accountant-facing export options at the practice level are narrow: **Xero HQ** lets a user with "manage staff" permission export a CSV of client/organisation information (names, trading/legal names, etc. — a client-list export, not a financial audit pack), while **Xero Practice Manager** can export essentially all its own practice data (clients, jobs, quotes, invoices) as XML via an emailed download link — neither is a per-client financial audit-pack export (TB/GL/ageing/fixed assets bundled) — [Xero Product Ideas: Practice Manager Client List - Export with all data](https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967127-practice-tools/suggestions/46193080-practice-manager-client-list-export-with-all-d); [Xero Central: Export data from Xero Partner Hub](https://central.xero.com/0/article/Export-Data).
- Sage 50's Audit Trail report itself is available from the Reports menu (Detailed Reports) with a selectable date range, showing date, type, category, value and who created each entry — functioning as a change-history export rather than a bundled audit pack of financial statements — [Sage Help: Using Sage 50's Audit Trail](https://help-sage50.na.sage.com/en-us/2019/Content/Reports_Forms/FinancialReports/Use_Audit_Trail.htm); Sage KB audit trail report pages (UK/US/IE variants) referenced in search results.

### Inferences
- Of the four, QuickBooks Online Accountant's Prep for Taxes is the closest thing to a purpose-built "audit/tax pack" — it is explicitly designed to reduce the work of gathering formal statements at year-end for an accounting professional's external review, which is functionally adjacent to (though not branded as) an audit pack.
- Xero and Zoho appear to expect the accountant to pull individual standard reports (Trial Balance, General Ledger, Aged Receivables/Payables, Fixed Asset reports, Bank Reconciliation Summary) separately rather than through one bundled "for auditors" export — this pushes bundling work onto external tools (e.g., audit software, Excel, or third-party practice-management add-ons).

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether Xero or Zoho Books offer any single combined "audit pack" export (as opposed to individual standard reports); no source directly addressed this framing for either product.
- Detailed confirmation of exact report names/paths for Fixed Asset registers and Bank Reconciliation reports in each app (beyond knowing they exist as standard reports in these platforms generally) was not obtained via primary sources in this research pass — this would need a follow-up fetch of each vendor's reports-catalogue help page.
- No information found on any built-in **audit sampling** feature (e.g., statistical/random transaction sampling for substantive testing) in any of the five apps — this appears to be a genuine, unaddressed gap in all of them, consistent with the assignment's framing that such functionality is left to external audit tools.

## Can an auditor leave queries/notes on transactions and get answers inside the app?

### Takeaway
Zoho Books is the only one of the four with clearly documented in-app commenting on financial records (comments/history on Banking transactions and manual journals, plus contextual "conversations" on custom module records); Xero's equivalent is its History & Notes feature, which is more of an audit-trail annotation (e.g., noting why a lock date was changed) than a two-way Q&A thread. No source found describes a dedicated auditor-to-preparer query/response workflow in QuickBooks Online or Sage.

### Cited Findings
- Zoho Books lets users **add comments and view history for transactions in the Banking module**, and a "Comments and History" section has been added to **manual journals** — enabling a running discussion thread attached to a specific record — [Zoho Books Release Notes](https://www.zoho.com/us/books/release-notes.html) (search-result excerpt).
- Zoho Books' **Customer Portal** supports real-time comment threads with customers on quotes/invoices/timesheets (with notification emails on comments) — this is a client-facing, not auditor-facing, feature, but shows the underlying commenting infrastructure exists in the product — [Zoho Books Help: Customer Portal](https://www.zoho.com/us/books/help/customer-portal/) (search-result excerpt).
- Zoho Books has extended contextual "conversations" to **custom module records**, letting a user ask questions and access record-specific details in-context — [Zoho Books What's New](https://www.zoho.com/us/books/whats-new.html) (search-result excerpt).
- Xero's **History and Notes** feature lets a user attach a note to an item (e.g., explaining why a lock date was unlocked), and this note becomes part of the permanent, date-stamped history for that record/settings area — this is note-attachment for audit-trail purposes rather than a threaded query/answer system between an external auditor and the business — [Xero Central: History and notes report](https://central.xero.com/0/article/View-a-history-and-notes-summary-for-transactions-and-user-activity) (search-excerpt); [Bookkeeping Tutor: Lock Dates in Xero](https://www.bookkeepingtutor.com.au/blog/lock-dates-in-xero/).

### Inferences
- None of the four apps appear purpose-built for a formal "auditor raises a query on transaction X, preparer responds in-thread, resolution is logged" workflow the way dedicated audit-management software would — Zoho Books comes closest structurally (comments + history on transactions) but its documented use cases are customer-facing (quotes/invoices) and banking/journal-specific, not framed as "auditor queries."
- This reinforces the assignment's implicit hypothesis that external audit-specific query/response workflows are a gap these apps leave to dedicated audit software or plain email/spreadsheet trackers.

### Gaps
- No source found confirms or denies a query/note/comment feature on general ledger transactions or invoices specifically for QuickBooks Online or Sage (50 or Business Cloud Accounting) — absence of evidence here, not confirmed absence of the feature.
- Could not verify whether Zoho Books' transaction-level comments are visible to/usable by a user in the **Accountant** role specifically (vs. only internal staff), which matters for whether an external auditor could actually use this channel.

## Does any app support proposed/draft adjusting entries from an accountant for the owner to approve?

### Takeaway
QuickBooks Online Accountant is the only one of the four with a clearly documented adjusting-entry workflow built for exactly this purpose (adjusting journal entries made inside Prep for Taxes, tracked alongside the client's P&L/balance sheet); no equivalent "propose then owner approves" adjustment workflow was found documented for Xero, Zoho Books, or Sage.

### Cited Findings
- In QuickBooks Online Accountant's **Prep for Taxes** dashboard, the accountant can make **adjusting journal entries** directly against the client's accounts at year end, with the tool tracking "account differences" and "adjusting entries you've made" alongside the P&L and balance sheet — [Intuit Tax Pro Center: Trial Balance in QBOA Reimagined to Prep for Taxes](https://accountants.intuit.com/taxprocenter/proconnect/trial-balance-in-qboa-reimagined-to-prep-for-taxes/); [Intuit: Introducing Prep for taxes](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-au/help-article/manage-client/introducing-prep-taxes/L46ItXRic_AU_en_AU).
- QuickBooks Online Accountant's **Reclassify Transactions** tool (batch move between accounts/classes/locations) functions as a bulk correction mechanism the accountant applies directly, rather than a proposal awaiting owner sign-off — meaning even QBO's adjustment tooling is generally accountant-applied-directly rather than propose-then-approve — [Webgility: How to Reclassify Transactions in QuickBooks Online](https://www.webgility.com/blog/how-to-reclassify-transactions-in-quickbooks-online).

### Inferences
- Across all four apps, the documented pattern is "the accountant/adviser role has the permission to directly post/adjust entries" rather than "the accountant drafts an entry that sits pending until the business owner clicks approve." A true two-step draft-then-owner-approval adjustment workflow does not appear to be a native feature in any of the five apps researched.
- This is consistent with these tools generally assuming the invited accountant is a trusted party (bookkeeper/CPA) who is expected to post directly, with the lock date / closing date mechanism (rather than an approval queue) serving as the main safeguard against unwanted changes once a period is considered final.

### Gaps
- No source found describing a formal "proposed adjusting entry pending owner approval" feature in Xero, Zoho Books, Sage 50, Sage Business Cloud Accounting, or mybooksai.
- No source found describing any formal **sign-off** workflow (e.g., an auditor or owner digitally "signing off" a period as reviewed/approved) in any of the five apps — if such a feature exists in any of them, it was not surfaced by the searches performed and would need dedicated documentation review (e.g., Xero's "Financial Statements" report approval workflow, if any, was not found).

## Summary of gaps left to external tools (cross-cutting)

### Takeaway
Across all five apps, three capabilities the assignment asked about were not found documented anywhere: (1) automatic/self-expiring external access, (2) built-in statistical/random audit sampling, and (3) a formal draft-adjustment-pending-owner-approval workflow with digital sign-off. These appear to be genuine, consistent gaps rather than a research shortfall, since multiple targeted searches across all vendors turned up nothing on these three points.

### Cited Findings
- (See per-question sections above for the individual absence-of-evidence findings on expiring access, sampling, and approval/sign-off workflows — repeated here as a cross-cutting synthesis point rather than a new citation.)

### Inferences
- Businesses and audit firms using any of these five apps for an external audit likely still need separate tools for: time-boxed access provisioning/de-provisioning, statistical sampling of transactions for substantive testing, formal engagement sign-off/documentation, and (for Xero/Sage/mybooksai in particular) a bundled one-click "audit pack" export — leaving QuickBooks Online Accountant's Prep for Taxes as the closest native approximation of an audit-support bundle among the five.

### Gaps
- A deeper, dedicated pass fetching each vendor's full reports catalogue and accountant-role permission matrix (rather than search-engine excerpts, several of which could not be corroborated by direct page fetch in this session due to fetch errors on central.xero.com and quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support pages) would strengthen confidence in several claims above, particularly: exact QBO audit log retention (2 vs 7 years), Xero Adviser role's precise permission boundaries, and Sage Business Cloud Accounting's (as opposed to Sage 50's) specific accountant-access and period-lock mechanics.
