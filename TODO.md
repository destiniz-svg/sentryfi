# Build plan

A complete, simple accounting product for founders and entrepreneurs.
A Maldives edition and an international edition, from one codebase.
Altura's own books are kept in it first, and prove it works.

Full history, rationale and design narrative behind every decision here: `docs/plan-history.md`.

## Fixes to come back to

Anything found and not fixed on the spot is written here the moment it is found, with where it is and why it waits (owner, 26 Sep 2026: "if we keep it for later we may not come up with it"). A line leaves only when it is fixed, saying so in the commit. Found on 26 Sep 2026:

**Wrong or unsafe**
- Found 26 Sep 2026 (tools/owed.js): a new order given a party by name, not picked, is matched to an existing contact whose name is only similar (`counterparties.findOrCreate`, trigram similarity 0.55 or more). "Check customer xpgnsl" became "Check customer xnwlcf". This is deliberate for bills read from paper (one supplier spelling itself two ways); for a name a person typed, two customers with close names can merge without anyone seeing. Decide: exact names only when typed (`exact: true` from order and invoice forms), or ask "Did you mean …?" first.

*Fixed 26 Sep 2026 (1.35.2):* a sale "from the main store" beyond what the places hold (that path could not be reached; it now refuses loudly instead of going below nothing); a count dated the day it is approved (now the day it was counted, or today with the counting date in the memo when that month is closed); zero-cost stock a dead end on arrival and on a job (a move worth nothing now stands with no entry, only then); the snapshot's raw date message; counts' add-an-item screen, cancel while counting, the unit under Counted, and the band's count as typed; the Items page's place line running into the next column and its 32px row buttons on phones.

**Missing pieces of what was built**
*Fixed 27 Sep 2026 (1.46.0):* regex escapes stripped by scripted edits (JS string literals turn d into d): the price tolerance in Approvals refused every percent (1.40.0), the write-down date and the audit question and adjustment dates were refused, the GST return month and year pickers were ignored, and the top bar split names on the letter "s". tools/match.js now changes the tolerance and tools/audit.js sets a due date, so these are exercised. See memory js-literal-eats-regex-escapes.
*Fixed 26 Sep 2026 (1.43.0):* samples draw from payments, receipts, credit notes and claims too; an invoice, credit note or receipt opens on its own document page; samples keep their seed, rule and population fingerprint and can be re-drawn and proved.
- Audit: the seal check reads the whole chain every time (it must, a break before the period breaks everything after). Fine now; slow on very large books.
- Audit: only the two people in a question (who asked, who was asked) can close it; a second auditor on the same engagement cannot close a colleague's question.
- Audit: which confirmation-request links were opened is shown only as "opened" with the time; the counterparty's IP and browser are kept on the reply but not shown on screen.
- Audit: the Count tab lists the company's counts within three weeks of the period end only; a count further away is attended by opening a period around it.
- Audit: what the auditor opened (each document, each file) is not itself logged for the company to see; only what they did (samples, questions, proposals, sign-off) is kept.
- Audit: confirmation emails go from Sentryfi's accounts address with the auditor as reply-to; a firm's own sending address (its domain) is not yet possible.
- Checks: tools/people.js is stale since Settings became rows (1.27.1): the People panel renders twice (phone and desk), so its strict locators fail. The access date on the People screen was checked by hand in a browser on 27 Sep 2026.
- Checks: tools/closing.js needs a bank account named "Checks Savings", which only the live Sentryfi Checks company has; locally it fails at that step.
- Checks: sign-in is limited to 30 per 15 minutes from one address, so running many browser checks back to back from one machine trips it (the second sign-in of a check then times out). Space them, or run them from the harness in fewer sign-ins.
- Worth less (1.41.0) is per item, not per batch or place: an expired batch cannot be written down on its own, only the whole item's average. Batches do not keep their own value.
- Worth less: what is still written down is replayed in the order moves were recorded; a sale entered later but dated before a write-down counts as after it.
- What looks wrong says "Worth less, on Items" for slow and expired stock, but does not link to it.
- Three-way match (1.40.0) checks only bills made from an order ("Make the bill" on the order). A bill recorded or scanned on its own and later tied to an order is not price-checked, and editing a held bill's amounts does not re-check it.
- Three-way match: anyone who can approve may accept a held bill, including the person who recorded it. Decide whether the accepter must be someone else (as counts do).
- Three-way match: the Bills list does not mark a held bill; it shows in Needs you and Approvals only.
- Promised and coming (1.38.0) is today's only: orders keep no history of how they stood, so the Stock page shows it only when looking at today.
- Promised and coming: reserved and free to sell are company-wide, not per place, and take no account of expired batches.
- Batches (1.37.0) are held company-wide, not by place: a sale at a site takes the earliest batch to expire anywhere, even if that batch is in the main store. Sending and arrivals do not name a batch. Decide per place once a company with batches has more than one store.
- Batches: goods back on a credit note, and stock found by a count, come in with no batch; a return to the supplier gives back the earliest to expire, not necessarily that bill's own batch.
- Batches: an expired batch can still be sold (What looks wrong warns). Decide whether to refuse it, together with item 37 (writing stock down).
- Batches: an item's history does not say which batch each move was.
- Packs (1.36.0): only bills, invoices, orders (through their line's unit) and counts speak in packs. Sending, arrivals, use on a job, opening stock and credit-note returns take the item's own unit only.
- Packs: after a bill's split is saved in boxes, reopening the split shows it in pieces (the stock line keeps pieces, not the unit it was said in). Right in the books, but surprising on screen.
- Packs: changing an item's own unit to the same name as its pack answers with the database's raw constraint message, not a plain one.
- Packs: purchase and sales order screens are not browser-checked in boxes (the server converts their lines; `tools/packs.js` covers bills, invoices and counts).
- A count into a closed month is not tested: the test suite has no closed period to post against (the path is in `counts.post`, `books_locked_through`).
- Arrivals: everyone who can receive sees every delivery, not only those going to places they look after. Chosen for small teams; revisit when a company has several sites.
- The Stock snapshot's limits (on the way over 7 days, not moving for 90) are fixed; no setting.
- The live "Sentryfi Checks" company has one person, so the spot-check and field-counter steps of `tools/counts.js` and the receiver steps of `tools/send-arrive.js` only run locally. Needs a second (site staff) person invited into Checks.

## Build order

The phases below say *what*; this says *when*. Set 23 September 2026. Each sprint ends deployed, checked in a real browser, and recorded here. Reorder when the owner says so, never silently.

**Sprint 1 — The phone, because that is where people are**

2. The main app on a phone: Home (cash card, thirty-day trend, Needs you), the Record sheet, Money (bills and invoices, swipe for the common action), More.
3. Bank lines on the phone, one card per line.
4. The expense manager's navigation settled (Home, Tin, Send, Owed back, Me), with Owed back for money paid out of pocket.

**Sprint 2 — A complete core**

8. Dimensions: project, branch, department, machine on any line.
9. Multi-currency finished: month-end revaluation, dollar invoices.
10. People finished: Face ID and fingerprint sign-in through passkeys, spending limits, password reset.
11. The security review that gates real money for companies other than Altura.

**Sprint 3 — Stock, the adviser, and imports (the owner's live problem)**

13. The adviser: stock or cost or asset, the charges on a document read, asked and learned.
14. Shipments and landed cost: freight by container, CBM or chargeable weight; the agent's disbursements; Customs as charged.

**Sprint 4 — The business modules**
15. Projects, construction first: budget, committed, claims and certification, retention, cost to complete, watched.
16. Purchase orders and sales orders, deliveries received against them.
17. Quotes, repeat billing, expense claims, approvals, payment runs, the customer portal.

**Sprint 5 and after**
18. The CFO and the morning brief.
19. Offline everywhere in the field; guided first-run; the documented interface for AI and other software.
20. Sellable: a second country pack, Dhivehi and right to left, sign-up and plans, the practice portal.
21. The expense companion app.

**Sprint 6 — Selling and buying, finished; stock in every place.** Set 26 September 2026 from the research in the Claude Doc (https://claude.ai/artifact/Eei6JPwaFbTZGyvvVDyQmp). In this order, smallest and most-used first:
22. **Duplicate** any quote, invoice, bill or order. *Done 26 Sep 2026:* invoices, quotes, sales and purchase orders, from the document or the order page; the form arrives filled in and nothing is saved until a person saves (tools/duplicate.js). Bills repeat under item 24.
23. **An accepted quote drafts its invoice**, and the office is told; a person always sends it (owner's decision). Then **billing a job in parts**, by percentage or milestone, with what is left shown on the quote. *Done 26 Sep 2026:* accepting a quote, in the office or by the customer's link, drafts its invoice (never posted or sent) and tells the people who record (tools/quote-invoice.js). A sales order is invoiced in parts by a percentage of the whole or an amount, named for its milestone; each part takes a share of every line, what is left shows on the order and on the quote, and asking for more than is left takes exactly the rest. A whole draft from the quote is discarded, by a person, to invoice in parts instead (tools/invoice-parts.js).
24. **Costs passed on**: a bill line or claim marked for a customer waits on their next invoice. **Repeating bills** (rent, internet) are drafted on schedule for a person to post. *Done 26 Sep 2026:* a bill's cost (on its split) or a claim's line can be charged to a customer, with a markup. Once the bill is posted or the claim approved, it waits on that customer's new invoice at cost plus markup, for a person to add; an invoice takes it once, and voiding the invoice lets it wait again. Repeating bills, on the Bills page, are drafted on their date onto their kind of cost and never posted by themselves (tools/pass-on.js).
25. **Stock places for real**: store, godown, outlet, site, factory, vehicle; a person in charge of each; a site tied to its project. Bills and deliveries receive into a named place, not only the main store. *Done 26 Sep 2026:* each place has a kind (store, godown, outlet, site, factory, vehicle), a person in charge from the company, and a site can belong to a project; tap a place on the Stock page to change it. A bill's split says which place its goods came into, and a delivery against a purchase order does too; the bill made from that order takes the delivery's place, and reversing or returning takes the goods back from there (tools/stock-places.js). Shipments still land in the main store.
26. **Send and arrive**: a transfer is two steps with goods in transit between them, received on a phone, and any shortfall is shown with a reason. **Use on a job**: stock issued to a project or department carries its cost there. *Done 26 Sep 2026:* stock sent to another place is on the way (held, at no place, and not sellable) until someone taps It arrived on the Stock page's On the way cards and says what came; a shortfall needs a reason and is written off at average cost to 5870 at the place it was going to, and more than was sent is refused. "It's there already" keeps the one-step move for the yard. Arrivals are their own append-only record (stock_arrivals) and are kept on the phone without signal. Use on a job takes stock from a place at average cost to the item's own kind of cost, or 5060 Materials used on jobs, tagged with the project and department; a site fills in its project (tools/send-arrive.js).
27. **The owner's stock snapshot**: what came in, what is where, what it is worth, what moved, what looks wrong. Every figure opens onto its moves. Value by place as at any date. *Done 26 Sep 2026:* Inventory > Stock (`/inventory`, read roles only) above Items. As at any date with a week or month: deliveries by supplier and place; each place's quantity and value (a place's share of the item's company-wide average; the last place takes the rounding, so places add up to the item), goods on the way as a place of their own; the total against the Stock account (1350) on that date; sold net of returns, used on jobs, transfers; and what looks wrong: short counts and arrivals, on the way over 7 days, below nothing at a place, no movement for 90 days, at or below reorder, and books that disagree. Every figure opens its moves (`GET /stock/moves`). Nothing stored: it is sums over the moves (tools/stock-snapshot.js). Limits of 7 and 90 days are fixed for now. Also 26 Sep: arrivals may be said by anyone who records, receives (procurement) or captures (site staff); field roles see a strip on their home board and an On the way board at /stock (pages/phone/Arrivals.jsx), quantities only, through `GET /stock/on-the-way`. Any receiver sees every delivery, not only those for places they are in charge of.
28. **Counting sessions** a place at a time, with differences shown before posting; a second unit (box and piece); batch and expiry where switched on. **Counts are blind** (owner, 26 Sep 2026, from the stock research): the counter never sees what the books say until they have submitted, and the book quantity is the one at the moment each line is counted, so moves during the count do not make false differences. Three kinds, one screen:
    - **Full count**: every item at a place (the year-end or quarter stocktake).
    - **Cycle count**: Sentryfi proposes today's few items per place from their value class, worked out from the moves (A, the items making most of the value, monthly; B quarterly; C yearly), so every item is counted through the year without closing the store.
    - **Spot check (surprise audit)**: the owner or accountant taps Spot check on a place; Sentryfi picks a handful of items at random, weighted towards value and towards items short before. It goes to someone other than the place's person in charge, is blind, and says nothing to the place in advance.
    - A difference within a tolerance (by value) posts as a count does, to 5870 with its reason. Above it, it waits for someone other than the counter to approve. Each place gets a count accuracy (share of lines within tolerance, over the last 90 days) on the owner's snapshot, and a place due or overdue for its cycle counts shows under What looks wrong.
    - *Counting done 26 Sep 2026 (1.35.0):* Inventory > Counts (/counts). stock_counts and stock_count_lines (ledger/counts.js): one open count per place; each line keeps the book figure at the moment it was counted, and nobody sees it until the count is submitted; within the company's tolerance (`companies.count_tolerance_laari`, MVR 500 unless changed; changed on the Counts page by whoever manages settings, 1.35.1) it posts at once, beyond it someone other than the counter approves, sends it back, or cancels. Cycle counts take up to 12 due items (A/B/C by value: 30/90/365 days since the last count, or since the item first came there); a spot check takes 5 at random, leaning to value and doubly to items short there in the last 180 days, and refuses the place's person in charge as counter. Field staff get "Full count at X · Count it" on their board and count there, with no money shown (tools/counts.js). *Item 28 done 26 Sep 2026.* *Batch and expiry done (1.37.0):* switched on per counted product (`stock_items.batches`); `stock_batches` (code, expiry, added never changed); a bill line says its batch and expiry (a draft may wait, posting will not); every move out that names no batch goes through `recordMove`, which takes the earliest to expire first and splits value and sale takings by quantity; a reversed bill takes back exactly its own batch. Items show the next batch out, the item sheet lists batches, the Stock page warns of expired and expiring within 30 days (tools/batches.js). Cost stays one weighted average per item. *Second unit done 26 Sep 2026 (1.36.0):* a product may also come in a pack (`stock_items.pack_unit`, `pack_size`); stock is kept in its own unit, and a bill, invoice or order line said in the pack is converted where each passes through (`billSplit.save`, `invoiceCost`, `stock.inBase`), so a pack size changed later changes no history. Items and the snapshot show "= 2 box 3 piece"; the item picker offers piece or box and prices the box; counts take boxes and loose pieces (tools/packs.js).
29. **Make**: a recipe turns raw material into finished goods, allowing for waste.
30. **Duty by HS line**: each Customs declaration line carries its HS code and duty, so duty is charged to its own goods.
31. **Set up by asking**: the AI interviews the owner, switches on only the stock features they need, and names the places. Moves can be said in plain words; the AI drafts them with a reason, and a person confirms.
32. **Between Sentryfi companies**: an invoice sent to another Sentryfi company arrives there as a draft bill.
33. **Paying online** through Bank of Maldives (and its wallet) and MIB, once the banks' merchant access arrives (see "Waiting on people").

*Added 26 Sep 2026 from the research in `docs/research/reports/Stock and inventory in leading apps.md`: controls that leading systems treat as standard and Sentryfi does not yet have. Build them straight after item 28, in this order, then 29 onwards.*
34. **Stock that is reserved and on order**: beside what is on hand, what is promised to open sales orders (reserved) and what is coming on approved purchase orders (on order), and available = on hand less reserved. Shown on Items, on the owner's snapshot, and on an order line as it is made. Sums over the moves and the orders, never stored. *Done 26 Sep 2026 (1.38.0):* `stock.committed`: reserved is what open sales orders have yet to invoice (posted invoices only; a draft leaves it spoken for), on order is what approved open purchase orders have yet to receive or bill, in the item's own unit. Free to sell = on hand less on the way and reserved. Items rows, the item picker (on sales, invoices and orders), and a Promised and coming list on today's Stock page, with Oversold under What looks wrong (tools/promised.js).
35. **What is still owed (backorders)**: the quantity left to deliver on each sales order and left to arrive on each purchase order, shown on the order and in one list; a line can be closed short, with a reason, to stop expecting the rest. *Done 26 Sep 2026 (1.39.0):* Inventory > Still owed (`/still-owed`, `GET /orders/owed`): to go out, to come in (late when past the expected date), and arrived waiting for the bill. On the order, each line says what is still to go or come, and a line that has moved at all can be closed short with its reason (`order_lines.closed_at`, `close_reason`; `POST /orders/:id/lines/:lineId/close`). A closed line wants only what has moved: order status, deliveries, reserved and on order, project commitments, open-order value in the CFO and analytics, and invoicing in parts all follow it (tools/owed.js).
36. **Bill matches what arrived (three-way match)**: per supplier, with an override per item, a bill is checked against the quantity ordered or the quantity received (the Odoo model, not a full approval chain). A bill for more than arrived, or at a price above the order beyond a tolerance, does not post; it waits in Needs you for a person to accept it with a reason or send it back. *Done 26 Sep 2026 (1.40.0):* a supplier's bills are for "what arrived" (the default) or "what was ordered" (paid ahead), on the supplier's form; an item can override it. A bill made from an order can only be for what that allows. A price more than the tolerance (2%, changeable in Approvals by whoever manages settings) above the order holds the bill: it will not post, shows in Needs you and Approvals, and is accepted there with a reason (`POST /bills/:id/match/accept`) or sent back (voided with the reason) (tools/match.js).*
37. **Writing stock down below cost (net realisable value, IAS 2)**: a move of its own, not a count, for damaged, expired or slow stock whose selling price less the cost to sell it is below its cost; the difference to its own expense account, with the reason and who decided. If the value recovers while the stock is still held, the write-down is reversed, never above the original cost. Stock not moving in 90 days and expired batches prompt the question under What looks wrong; a person decides. *Done 26 Sep 2026 (1.41.0):* "Worth less" on an item (for those who approve) takes what one will fetch now and why; the difference goes between Stock on hand and 5880 Stock written down as a value-only move ('written_down'). Written back up only as far as the write-down on stock still held (a sale takes its share of it), never above cost (`stock.worth`, `stock.writeDown`, tools/worth.js).*

*Added 26 Sep 2026 (owner): the financial auditor's workspace, after items 34–37.*
38. **Audit**: a workspace for the external auditor (the audit firm, a bank's reviewer, MIRA), on the existing Auditor role (reads everything and the trail, changes nothing). It feeds the auditor's own tools and does not replace them: no working-paper files, risk matrices or materiality. Nothing in it posts; the company decides. In this order, the first three first because they need almost nothing new:
    - **A period to audit**, usually a closed year, with the seal checked when it opens: every entry intact since it was posted, or exactly which one is not.
    - **Samples**: random, or by value (every bill over an amount, so many invoices at random), from any document kind. Each opens its document, entry, payment and attachments, and is ticked as seen with a note. The sample is kept, so the same one can be shown again.
    - *Done 26 Sep 2026 (1.42.0): the first two.* Accounting > Audit (/audit): a period opened with its seal checked (the whole chain read; problems split into inside the period and elsewhere), and samples of bills, invoices or journal entries, so many at random or every one over an amount, kept with what each said when drawn. Each item opens onto its document, entry by account, money paid or received, and papers; "Seen, next" ticks it with a note and goes on. A new capability, "audit", for the auditor (and owner and accountant), writes only the workspace's own tables; the auditor still cannot record anything (test/audit.test.js, tools/audit.js).
    - **The audit pack**: one download for the period, with trial balance, general ledger, receivables and payables ageing at the year end, the asset register with depreciation, stock valuation by place at the year end, bank reconciliations at each close, and GST returns filed. Spreadsheet files plus the issued copies of documents.
    - **Questions to the company**: a question pinned to an entry or document, answered in the app by the accountant or owner, with open and answered ones listed (on the existing comment threads).
    - **Confirmations**: the auditor asks a customer or supplier to confirm their balance at the year end through their portal link; their answer or difference comes back into the workspace.
    - **Watching the year-end count**: the auditor starts or watches a blind count (item 28) and sees its differences.
    - **Proposed adjustments**: the auditor drafts a correcting entry with its reason. It waits as a draft for the accountant or owner to accept (posted in the open period, dated and named) or reject, and the auditor sees which.
    - *Done 26 Sep 2026 (1.43.0):* samples keep a seed (made at the draw, never chosen), their rule and a fingerprint of the population, and "Prove it" re-draws them: the same items, or what changed. Five ways to draw: at random, every one over an amount, key items and the rest at random, by monetary unit (MUS), and from the journal risks. Seven kinds: bills, invoices, entries, payments, receipts, credit notes, claims. The journal risk screen (ISA 240) reads every entry in the period against ten signs (posted after the period, reversed just after, backdated, not from a document, a seldom poster, out of hours or the Maldivian weekend, round amounts, no description, a seldom-used account, the last three days), ranks them and draws from them. ### Phase B — The adviser and imports

**The adviser.** Nothing posts unguided: on every document it decides stock vs cost vs asset, whether it belongs to an open shipment, the tax treatment, timing and duplicates — asking at most one question per document, in plain words, and remembering the answer. Includes the seventh question, reading each charge on a Customs notice or agent's invoice line by line, and the read-ask-learn rule: a charge it does not recognise is asked about once and remembered against that supplier and wording. It proposes; a person accepts; it never posts. *(was step 26b)*
**Done when:** a founder can record a container, a freight bill, an insurance premium and a laptop, each landing correctly with a reason shown, and the app asks at most four questions in total.

**Imports and landed cost.** A shipment collects every cost against it — invoice, freight, insurance, duty, bank charges, inland transport — and spreads them across the goods by value, weight, volume or count, covering FCL, LCL by CBM and air by chargeable weight. Disbursements paid on the importer's behalf are told apart from the agent's own supply. The Customs-assessed figure is recorded as charged, never recalculated or questioned. *(was step 23a)*
**Done when:** an owner opens one shipment and sees what it actually cost landed, per unit, against the invoice price — and a month later the stock on the balance sheet agrees.

### Phase F — On top

**The CFO: watches, guides, advises.** Asked for on 23 September 2026, and it absorbs what was "the watching layer" (old step 34) and the analytics of D4. An advisor built into the product that learns this business and keeps learning, and brings the outside world to it:

- **It learns the business.** From the ledger and the answers the owner gives: what the company sells, to whom, its suppliers, its seasons, its margins by line, its cost structure, how it is financed. The profile is visible and correctable, and it grows with every month of books.
- **It watches.** Continuous reconciliation, anomalies raised the week they happen, and four figures kept apart: cash now, expected in, committed out, and a forecast.
- **It reads the market, here and abroad, and translates it into this company's numbers.** Maldives: MMA and Customs exchange rates, MIRA and Customs changes, the gazette, fuel and freight into Malé, tourist arrivals for anyone whose customers depend on them. Abroad: freight indices, commodity prices for what the company imports, exchange rates, interest rates. A market note says what changed and what it does to *this* business: "freight from Jebel Ali is up about 12% — your next container of fittings costs about MVR 4,000 more landed, and LED margin falls from 22% to 19%."
- **Every morning, a brief.** Three minutes, at a time the owner chooses, in the app and as a push where the phone allows, and readable aloud in Dhivehi or English for the drive to site: what changed since yesterday, what to do today, one market note in the company's own figures, one thing learned. Drawn on the canvas as "The CFO's morning brief".
- **Practical and creative.** Specific actions with a figure attached, never generic advice; a scenario on request ("what if I buy the second excavator?"); a question answered from the books with the entries behind it.

The rules that do not bend: every figure opens onto its entries, and every market claim carries its source and date — an advisor that cannot show its working is guessing. It advises and drafts; it never posts. It says when it is unsure. It is a skill the product grows: its knowledge of each company lives in the company's own records, and its knowledge of the world is refreshed daily, shared across companies so it is gathered once. Built on the Claude API with the model chosen per job — a small model to gather and summarise, a larger one to advise — so a brief costs cents, not dollars.
**Done when:** an owner reads the brief every morning because it is worth reading: it caught something before month end, put a market change into their own figures, and taught them one thing — and every claim in it could be checked.

**The expense companion app.** A separate, lighter app for capturing personal or team expenses on the move, feeding the same ledger through the same door as everything else — for people who need expense capture without the full accounting product.
**Done when:** an expense captured in the companion app appears in the main books with nothing re-entered.

## Rules of the work

- **Gated commits.** Lint, build and tests must pass before anything lands.
- **Every build that goes live is a release.** Add it at the top of `backend/src/releases.js` with the next version (1.14 for new things, 1.13.1 for fixes alone), a title, a one-line summary and each change as new, improved or fixed with where it opens. It shows on What's new (`/whats-new`, public at `/updates`), the menu shows the version and build, and the bell hears about it in the Sunday round-up, to each person only what their role can use; mark a release `headline: true` to announce it the day it ships. `test/releases.test.js` refuses a version out of order.
- **Verify every UI change in a real browser** — `tools/*.js` against a live Chrome, not a guess from the code.
- **One thing at a time, finished.** The old store is removed in the same step that replaces it; two record stores never coexist longer than one step.
- **Nothing posts or sends without a person.** Capture, connectors, accepted quotes, repeating bills and the AI all make drafts. A person posts or sends them.
- **Tax rules are configuration, never code.** A new country is a pack, not a rewrite of the ledger.
- **Nothing is deleted.** A correction is a reversing entry with a reason; the original stays.
- **One step at a time.** Keep this file current — an item leaves "the plan" when it is done and says so in "Where we are".
- **Nothing found is left unwritten.** A bug, gap or rough edge not fixed on the spot goes into "Fixes to come back to" the moment it is found, with where it is and why it waits. Pre-existing ones too. A report that says "noted" must point at the line.
- **Token efficiency.** Cheaper models for research and bulk rewrites; read only the part of a file you need; use `docs/domain/*` instead of re-researching what is already settled.
