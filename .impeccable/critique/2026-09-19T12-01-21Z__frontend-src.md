## Design Specificity Verdict

An unrelated product could ship this authenticated app unchanged, and on the auth screens it literally still is one. Three products in three skins, separable by file: a landing page authored for Altura in detail; login and register still carrying the purchased product's teal gradient and an "AI Invoice Manager" badge; and between them generic freelancer invoicing with a yellow repaint.

Evidence: expense categories are General, Software, Hosting, Meals, Travel, Office, Marketing, Equipment, with no construction cost code and an Adobe Inc. placeholder. Payment methods offer PayPal and cheques. formatMoney defaults to USD and is called without a currency on every dashboard and report tile. The dashboard empty state reads "let us get you paid" for a business whose problem is bills to pay. ## What's Working

ErrorPage is the best-written screen in the product, answering the one question a finance user has before offering the two actions that help, with the stack trace folded away honestly. It uses yellow with ink text at 11.9:1, the pattern the rest of the app gets wrong.

MobileNav is a properly built dialog: focus trap, Escape bound to the window, scroll lock, focus restore, safe-area insets, 56px targets, reduced-motion guard. It is the reference implementation, which is what makes the four money modals' omissions stand out.

Loading and empty-state discipline is on every list page, with distinct copy for no-results versus nothing-yet.

## Persona Red Flags

Alex, power user: no bulk operations at all, on a product whose story is clearing a backlog. One keyboard shortcut. A React Query v4 option on a v5 project, so the list blanks on every keystroke. No autosave, no dirty guard.

Sam, screen reader and keyboard: no skip link. The only h1 on any page is the greeting, so the heading outline says every page is called Hello. No live region anywhere, so save, delete, payment and error are silent. About fifteen icon-only buttons have no accessible name. Every focus ring computes between 1.05 and 2.74:1, all below the 3:1 floor.

Casey, one-handed mobile: MobileNav is good; above it, 56px rows contain 28px action buttons, so open and delete sit pixels apart under one thumb. Four modals are centred boxes with no sheet treatment or scroll lock. No camera path: a file picker, not a shutter.

Abdulla, the owner: no place to file GST, no deadline, no period status, no MIRA figures, no checklist. The word bill does not appear; no payables concept, so his hardest job has no screen. No cash boxes, bank balances, Money In, directors ledger or projects. The dashboard leads with Total Revenue in US dollars.

## Questions to Consider

If the landing page is the only file that knows what this product is, why is it the only one that will be thrown away? It holds more domain truth in 480 lines than the 5,000 behind the login.

The design system names the moment of commitment as the signature interaction, and the web is where the books change. Why did the desk register inherit the colours and the radii but not the reassurance?

Seven navigation slots, none of which is GST, bills, cash or projects. Take the chassis, leave the domain: the chassis was taken, the domain came with it, and the nav bar is where it is now load-bearing.
